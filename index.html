Personal Google Drive Access

This is an informational page for a private OAuth client used with rclone, an open-source file-management program.

The client allows its owner to authorize rclone to access personal Google Drive accounts for browsing, transferring, and managing files from a local computer. It is not a public web application or a service offered to others.

The website does not ask for Google passwords, store Google Drive files, or provide account sign-in functionality. Authorization is handled directly through Google by the local rclone application.
