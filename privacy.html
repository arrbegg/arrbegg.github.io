Privacy Policy

This OAuth client is used privately by its owner to connect the open-source rclone program to Google Drive accounts the owner controls.

Google data access

When the owner authorizes access, rclone may read, upload, modify, move or delete Google Drive files depending on the permissions granted and commands performed. rclone communicates with Google's APIs from the owner's computer.

Storage and sharing

This website does not collect Google account passwords or Google Drive data, and does not sell or share Google Drive data. rclone stores authorization credentials and any locally cached file data on the owner's computer. No Google Drive data is intentionally sent to this website.

Website hosting

This static website has no tracking scripts, analytics, or contact forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary website access information under its privacy statement.

Control and revocation

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in their Google Account's third-party access settings and can delete local rclone credentials and cached files.
